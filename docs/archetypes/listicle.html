Seven steps, and none of them is a search

The whole pipeline is arithmetic on numbers that were fixed months ago.

Steps 1–4

Your words become numbers

  1. 01Tokenise — your text is split into sub-word pieces.
  2. 02Embed — each piece becomes a vector of a few thousand numbers.
  3. 03Attend — every token looks at every earlier token and reweighs it.
  4. 04Stack — that happens again through dozens of layers.
@rlnorthcutt
3 / 8
Steps 5–7

Numbers become your answer

  1. 01Score — the last layer scores every token in the vocabulary.
  2. 02Sample — one token is drawn from that distribution.
  3. 03Repeat — the token is appended and the whole pass runs again.
