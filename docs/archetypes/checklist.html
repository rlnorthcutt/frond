The prompt checklist I actually use

Ten lines, split into what to do and what to stop doing.

Why

A prompt is context, not incantation.

The model has no memory of you, no access to your files and no way to check itself.

@rlnorthcutt
2 / 8
Do

Five things worth typing

  • State the reader and the decision they are making
  • Paste the source material into the prompt
  • Name the format and the length
  • Give one worked example of a good answer
  • Ask what it would need to be sure
@rlnorthcutt
3 / 8
Do not

Five things that waste tokens

  • Telling it not to hallucinate
  • Asking it to be creative with no constraint
  • Stacking six questions in one message
  • Trusting a citation you have not opened
  • Assuming it remembers yesterday
